About this connection

Whisper large-v3-turbo runs on the laptop's NVIDIA GPU. Recordings are processed locally on that computer. This service does not save an audio or transcript history. Processing is limited to one recording at a time, up to five minutes or 16 MiB.

Language-model cleanup is not enabled. The text shown is the speech model's transcript.
